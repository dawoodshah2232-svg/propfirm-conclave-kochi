About the conclave

Built for traders
who want funding.

Not another generic finance expo. PropFirm Conclave is a focused, two-day gathering built around one idea: connecting skilled traders with the capital that can scale them.

Why it exists

The funded-trading movement needed a home in India.

Thousands of Indian traders have the skill to trade — but not the capital. Proprietary trading firms changed that equation: pass an evaluation, prove your risk discipline, and trade firm capital with a share of the profits.

Yet most traders learn about prop firms from scattered videos and forum threads. PropFirm Conclave brings the firms, the funded traders and the educators into one room — so you can ask, compare and learn face-to-face.

  • Education first — honest sessions on how evaluations, drawdowns and payouts really work.
  • Direct access — meet the teams behind the challenges, not just their ads.
  • Community — Kerala's trader community is growing fast; this is where it gathers.

What is prop trading?

Funded trading, in plain words.

New to the concept? Here's the 60-second version.

1 · The evaluation

A prop firm gives you a demo account with a profit target (often 8–10%) and strict risk rules — like a maximum daily loss and overall drawdown. Hit the target without breaking the rules, usually across one or two phases.

2 · The funded account

Pass, and you trade the firm's capital — accounts typically from $5,000 up to $200,000+, with scaling plans that grow your capital as you stay consistent.

3 · The profit split

You keep a large share of the profits you generate — commonly 70–90% — paid out on a regular cycle. The firm takes the rest for providing the capital and infrastructure.

Honest note: most traders don't pass evaluations on the first attempt — risk discipline, not strategy, is usually the reason. That's exactly why the conclave dedicates full sessions to risk management. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
