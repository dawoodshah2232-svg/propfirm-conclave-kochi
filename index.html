The event

Where traders meet the firms that fund them.

PropFirm Conclave is India's focused gathering for the funded-trading movement — two days where aspiring and professional traders sit across the table from proprietary trading firms, learn how evaluations really work, and build the skills to trade firm capital.

  • Face time with prop firms — ask about challenges, payouts and rules directly.
  • Funded-trader playbooks — risk, psychology and consistency from traders who passed.
  • Live trading & workshops — watch strategies executed in real market conditions.
