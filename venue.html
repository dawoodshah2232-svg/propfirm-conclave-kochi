Getting there

Easy to reach, worth the trip.

  • By air: Cochin International Airport (COK) connects to all major Indian cities and the Gulf — the venue is a short drive away.
  • By rail: Angamaly and Aluva stations are nearby, with Kochi's metro extending toward Aluva.
  • By road: Right on NH 544 — easy access from Kochi city, Thrissur and Kottayam.
  • Stay: Hotels across budgets near Angamaly, Aluva and Kochi city — plus the backwaters of Fort Kochi for a weekend extension.
