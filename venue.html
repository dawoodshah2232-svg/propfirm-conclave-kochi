Why Adlux

A venue built for
big-stage events.

A prop trading conclave needs three things: a main stage with real presence, a proper expo floor for prop firms, and easy access for traders flying in. Adlux delivers all three — and it has the national recognition to prove it.

The main convention block · Adlux, Angamaly
Evenings at Adlux · event-ready grounds
Right on the highway · hard to miss

Nationally awarded

Named India's Best Stand-Alone Convention Centre by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.

60,000 sq ft expo hall

A dedicated exhibition centre for the prop-firm expo floor — booths, demo zones and live trading desks.

Halls for every format

Four halls seating ~1,000 to ~3,000 — keynotes in the gallery-seated Utpal, focused sessions in the acoustic King's Court.

15 minutes from COK

Cochin International Airport is a short drive away — easy for outstation and Gulf-based traders flying in.

Room for everyone

Parking for 500+ cars and 1,000 bikes, plus 48 in-house guest rooms.

Right on NH 544

Direct highway access from Kochi city, Thrissur and Kottayam — no navigating town traffic.

Getting there

Easy to reach, worth the trip.

  • By air: Cochin International Airport (COK) connects to all major Indian cities and the Gulf — the venue is a short drive away.
  • By rail: Angamaly and Aluva stations are nearby, with Kochi's metro extending toward Aluva.
  • By road: Right on NH 544 — easy access from Kochi city, Thrissur and Kottayam.
  • Stay: Hotels across budgets near Angamaly, Aluva and Kochi city — plus the backwaters of Fort Kochi for a weekend extension.
