Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

PropFirm Conclave Kochi 2026 is organised by Finfluenze ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact details you share via forms or email (name, email, message) — used only to respond to your enquiry.
  • Ticket information if you book (name, email, ticket type) — used for event access and communication.
  • Technical data — anonymised analytics (pages visited, device type) to improve the site.

3. How we use it

To respond to enquiries, manage ticket bookings, send event updates you've asked for, and improve the website. We never sell your personal data.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies: a language-preference cookie for the translator, and privacy-respecting analytics. No advertising trackers.

5. Third parties

Data may be processed by our ticketing provider and email tools solely to deliver the event. Google Translate may process page text when you switch languages.

6. Your rights

You can ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data at any time by emailing events@finfluenze.com.

7. Data retention

Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months; ticket records as required for accounting and event operations.

8. Changes

We'll post any updates to this page with a revised date.
